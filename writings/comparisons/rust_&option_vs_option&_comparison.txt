Optimization to pointer
	-Note, the optimization of changing "link perhaps" for whatever constantness to a pointer is with the assumption that the rare case of a link to address zero isn't needed.
		-for "link perhaps"
			-applies to all forms
			-number of states: (t-states*addresses + 1)
	-Similarly, "pointer perhaps" and "perhaps pointer" are optimized to a pointer by the assumption that some(NULL) and a pointer that isn't NULL to a perhaps that is none() isn't needed.
		-for "pointer perhaps"
			-applies to all forms
			-number of states: (t-states*(addresses-1) + 2)
		-for "perhaps pointer"
			-applies when the perhaps is immutable or constant
			-number of states: ((t-states+1)*(addresses-1) + 1)
	-The optimization for "perhaps link" assumes both
		-for "perhaps link"
			-applies when the perhaps is immutable or constant
			-number of states: ((t-states+1)*addresses)
	-Number of states for a pointer: (t-states*(addresses-1) + 1)
